Notifications

Enable notifications

Turn on browser push alerts for this device. If Chrome says permission was not granted: click the lock icon left of the address bar → Site settings → Notifications → Allow, then toggle again. On iPhone use Safari → Add to Home Screen (Chrome on iPhone cannot send push alerts).

Timezone

Used for all timed alerts on this account (for you and for your partner settings). Frequency and send times are set separately on each alert below. Instant alerts (e.g. period just logged) are sent right away.

For myself

Alerts on this device about your own cycle. “Days before” means reminders start that many days ahead and repeat every day until the day itself. For period, if you haven’t logged a start yet, reminders continue with “X days late” (up to 14 days).

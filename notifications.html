Notifications

Enable notifications

Turn on browser push alerts for this device. If Chrome says permission was not granted: click the lock icon left of the address bar → Site settings → Notifications → Allow, then toggle again. On iPhone use Safari → Add to Home Screen (Chrome on iPhone cannot send push alerts).

When to send

Timed alerts (period / fertile / safe windows) use these hours in your timezone. Instant alerts (e.g. period just logged) are sent right away. Turn on “Enable notifications” above (and allow Chrome) so pushes actually arrive.

For myself

Alerts on this device about your own cycle. “Days before” means reminders start that many days ahead and repeat every day until the day itself.

For partner

Types you allow to be sent to your partner. They must also enable notifications on their device and opt in to each type. “Days before” reminders repeat every day until the event.

Receive from partner

Choose which alerts you want on this device. Your partner must also enable the matching “For partner” type.

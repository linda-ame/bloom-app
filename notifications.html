Notifications

Enable notifications

Turn on browser push alerts for this device. On iPhone/iPad, add Bloom to your Home Screen first, then allow notifications.

When to send

Timed alerts (period / fertile / safe windows) use these hours in your timezone. Instant alerts (e.g. period just logged) are sent right away.

For myself

Alerts on this device about your own cycle.

For partner

Types you allow to be sent to your partner. They must also enable notifications on their device and opt in to each type.

Receive from partner

Choose which alerts you want on this device. Your partner must also enable the matching “For partner” type.
